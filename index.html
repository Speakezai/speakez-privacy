SpeakEZ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

Thank you for using SpeakEZ ("we," "us," "our," or "SpeakEZ"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the SpeakEZ mobile application (the "App").

By using SpeakEZ, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your profile picture (if you choose to upload one)
  • Sign-in information if you use Google or Apple Sign-In

Voice Coaching Data

  • Voice input: When you speak during practice sessions, your speech is transcribed to text in real time and analyzed to provide coaching feedback. We do not store audio recordings of your voice. Only the text transcription and the resulting analysis (such as traits, coaching focus areas, and scores) are saved to your account.
  • Practice session history: We store records of your practice sessions, including session type, scores, and related coaching data, so you can track your progress over time.
  • Saved phrases and words: Any phrases or words you choose to save to your Word Bank are stored in your account.

Device Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions:

  • Microphone: Required to capture your speech during practice sessions for real-time transcription and coaching.
  • Photo Library: Requested only if you choose to upload a profile picture or select a file (such as a resume) to enhance your coaching experience.
  • Notifications: Requested if you enable reminders within the App.

You can manage these permissions at any time through your device's Settings.

Automatically Collected Information

We may collect limited technical information such as app usage patterns and error logs to help us maintain and improve the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the App's coaching features
  • Generate personalized coaching feedback using AI analysis (see Section 3)
  • Save your progress, practice history, and Word Bank content
  • Communicate with you, including responding to support requests
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the App
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

To power certain features, SpeakEZ shares limited data with the following third-party service providers:

  • OpenAI: Text transcriptions and related context (such as your practice question or scenario) are sent to OpenAI's API to generate coaching analysis and feedback. OpenAI processes this data according to their own privacy and data usage policies.
  • Firebase (Google): We use Firebase for authentication, data storage, and app infrastructure. Firebase processes and stores your account data and app data on our behalf.
  • Google Sign-In / Apple Sign-In: If you choose to sign in using Google or Apple, those providers share limited profile information (such as your name and email) with us to create and manage your account.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Your data is stored securely using Firebase's infrastructure. We retain your account information and practice history for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account (see Section 6), we permanently delete your associated data from our systems.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: Review the information associated with your account within the App (Profile, Word Bank, Voice Archive).
  • Request a copy of your data: Contact us at the email below to request an export of your information.
  • Delete your account: You may permanently delete your account and associated data at any time via Profile → Privacy & Data → Delete My Account within the App. This action is irreversible.
  • Manage permissions: You may enable or disable microphone, photo library, and notification permissions at any time through your device Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

SpeakEZ is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above and, where appropriate, through notice within the App.
